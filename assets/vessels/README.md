# Vessel paintings

Six transparent 1536 × 1024 PNG illustrations generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The cutter established the material rendering and palette; the remaining vessels used it as a style reference. Original generated files were copied into this directory. Both the dock and shipyard use these same plates.

Files: `cutter.png`, `sloop.png`, `carrack.png`, `barque.png`, `brig.png`, `dhow.png`.

## Cutter prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished high-resolution transparent-background ship illustration for a premium historical maritime strategy game, to be used both as a large dock inspection hero and as a shipyard catalog image.
Subject: ONE compact European merchant cutter: one tall gaff-rigged mast, a large quadrilateral mainsail aft, two triangular foresails toward a long slender bowsprit. Narrow deep mahogany wooden clinker hull, restrained brass trim, a small aft cabin with glazed windows, curved stern, visible rudder, deck cargo hatch, coiled lines, railings and a tiny muted burgundy pennant. No guns on this peaceful trader.
Camera/composition: strict side elevation, stern on LEFT and pointed bow on RIGHT, only a very slight elevated angle to reveal the deck; complete ship visible, including all rigging, mast top, bowsprit and rudder. Centered on a 3:2 landscape canvas, occupies 90% of width and 86% of height with only small safe transparent margins. Ship silhouette is clean and readable.
Art direction: exquisite hand-painted museum-quality nautical illustration, sophisticated AAA strategy-game art direction. Realistic craftsmanship and construction, delicate engraved linework, ink-and-watercolor washes, richly shaded timber with fine plank grain, taut believable rigging and ratlines, warm ivory sailcloth with subtle seams, folds and weathering. Volumetric, tactile and realistic, NOT a flat vector icon, cartoon, paper cutout, toy or low-poly model. Warm light from upper left, restrained chiaroscuro with fine readable detail.
Palette: antique ivory, warm umber, burnt sienna, desaturated sea-green details, subtle oxidized brass; integrates with antique parchment UI.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha. Absolutely NO paper rectangle, sky, scenery, water, sea, clouds, cast shadow below the ship, labels, letters, title, border or watermark. One ship only.

## Remaining vessel prompt set

Shared direction: create a new vessel using the cutter as a style reference only. Strict side elevation, stern left and bow right; entire ship visible on a 3:2 transparent canvas. Museum-quality nautical ink-and-watercolor illustration with realistic timber, fine rigging, weathered ivory sails, warm upper-left light and antique brass. No vector or cartoon treatment. No paper, sky, water, glow, scenery, shadow, text, border or watermark. Use the following subject for each separate asset:

### sloop

ONE light and fast courier sloop, slim low freeboard dark walnut hull, tiny aft cabin, one mast carrying a tall lateen triangular ivory sail on a long diagonal yard, a slender pointed bow and long bowsprit, modest brass rails and muted blue-green trim. Clearly smaller, leaner and more agile than the cutter reference. No guns.

### carrack

ONE magnificent deepwater merchant carrack, broad deep oak hull with massive cargo capacity, high stepped aftcastle and glazed captain's gallery, raised forecastle, THREE tall masts carrying tiered billowing square ivory sails and a smaller aft lateen sail, complex ratlines and standing rigging, long bowsprit with a staysail. Restrained weathered brass and oxblood trim; powerful ocean-going silhouette, not a warship.

### barque

ONE elegant long survey barque, narrow long dark teak hull, low clean deck with modest chart cabin at stern, THREE tall masts: fore and main square-rigged with stacked ivory sails, aft mast fore-and-aft gaff rig. Slender bowsprit, restrained teal trim and antique brass, fine complex ratlines. Clearly sleeker and lower than a carrack. No gun battery.

### brig

ONE formidable armed merchant brig, sturdy mahogany hull of medium depth, TWO tall square-rigged masts with tiers of weathered ivory sails, long bowsprit with triangular staysails, a distinct row of six dark iron gunports in the near hull with visible bronze gun barrels, small raised aft cabin, navy-green trim and warm brass fittings. Believable restrained military silhouette.

### dhow

ONE graceful Indian Ocean trading dhow, curved warm teak hull, very strongly raked pointed bow, swept raised stern, small open aft shelter, ONE mast with a very large diagonal lateen yard and single triangular ivory sail, minimal stays, coiled ropes, woven deck cargo sacks and a small cargo hatch, understated faded terracotta trim. Distinctively curved Arabian merchant silhouette. No guns.
