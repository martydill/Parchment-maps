# Opening cinematic materials

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool using the imagegen skill. The
original PNGs were converted to JPEG with macOS `sips` for delivery. Both
materials are embedded by `scripts/assemble-single-html.mjs` when packaging.

- `table-weathered.jpg`: cracked, scratched oak tabletop; fills the viewport
  without stretching the grain.
- `parchment-weathered.jpg`: blank aged paper; multiplied over the game's
  starting view and rolled surfaces, then faded during the camera approach.

## Table prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen background material
texture for a sailing game's map-opening animation. Generate a landscape 3:2
image, straight overhead orthographic view, filled edge-to-edge by an ancient
heavily used oak captain's worktable built from five broad horizontal planks of
unequal widths. No map or objects. Extremely old matte weathered wood, warm
grey-brown oak with rubbed bare pale areas, ingrained black grime, jagged splits
following the grain, deeply scored knife marks, long scratches, dents, irregular
dark knots, chipped plank seams, a few old forged nail heads and subtle old cup
stains. Distinct uneven natural grain, centuries of use, tactile rough wood.
Museum photograph or realistic AAA game texture, soft diffuse warm side light
from upper left, moderate exposure with readable natural texture across the
whole surface. The table must look dry, abused and aged rather than polished or
varnished. No text, no border, no tools, no coins, no ornaments, no vignette, no
table outer edges; only wood surface.

## Paper prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: unprinted parchment surface
material used as a multiply texture on top of a real game map. Generate a
landscape 4:3 image, orthographic close overhead scan, old blank parchment surface
extending edge to edge of the image. Very old worn warm ochre ivory parchment,
visible mottled organic fibres and paper grain, uneven cloudy yellowing, broad
smoky brown handling smudges near margins, irregular dried water tide marks, tiny
black dirt flecks, rubbed pale abrasions, faded intersecting creases and short
splits where folds meet. Heavy burnt-brown frayed-looking margin staining, but
retain an ivory luminous middle so ink underneath remains readable. Texture of a
sailor's chart used for decades; tactile realistic historical material, not
stylized. Completely blank: absolutely no writing, map symbols, lines of
printing, graphics, coastline, letters, logos or text. The image is entirely the
paper material with no background, no border frame, no objects, no cast shadow,
no lit specular sheen, no modern pristine sheet.
