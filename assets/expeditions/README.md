# Expedition illustrations

Three 960 × 720 WebP illustrations generated with the built-in imagegen tool and optimized for the expedition cards. The shared art direction uses the game's sepia contours, muted watercolor palette, and warm parchment. These are decorative approach illustrations, independent of a site's terrain.

Files: `scout.webp`, `expedition.webp`, `deep.webp` (about 500 KB total).

## Prompt set

### scout

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: production game card artwork, one landscape 4:3 illustration with no lettering, no UI, no frame.
Primary request: a beautiful hand-painted expedition illustration for Parchment Maps, a nautical merchant sailing game whose world is an antique parchment map. The art must look drawn into a cartographer's sketchbook, using thin warm umber ink contour lines and fine crosshatching, restrained translucent watercolor washes on warm yellowed ivory paper. Stylized simplified shapes like miniature map illustrations, delicate paper grain, no photographic rendering, no dramatic videogame lighting, no slick digital painting, no oil paint. Colors: parchment #ceb584 and #f0dfb4, sepia ink #6d5940, muted sage #718367, dusty sea teal #8baba1, slate #657378, tiny ochre touches #c9a568. Earthy, low contrast, beautiful intentional line work, readable silhouette, large clear composition. Edge of scene gently dissolves into unpainted warm parchment rather than a hard rectangle. Nautical age-of-sail clothing. Do not depict a specific named location or futuristic/supernatural elements. No text, no labels, no symbols overlaid, no watermarks. Scene: a lone sailor in a simple coat and tricorne stands on a low coastal bluff, back three-quarter view, using a brass telescope to survey an unfamiliar sheltered cove. Small two-masted merchant vessel at anchor in the middle distance, gentle stylized waves, pale distant islands and sky. Quiet daylight, the landscape is more prominent than the person. The person takes at most one third of the composition. The drawing communicates reconnaissance and a short shore visit.
