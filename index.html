The Gilded Archipelago

✦ ☙ ⚓ ❧ ✦

You inherit a narrow-hulled merchant vessel, a weathered chart, and a debt owed to the Crown of Avelorn.

Your voyage begins at Goldhaven, your home port on the coast of Avelorn.

To sail, drag the touch wheel toward the place you want the ship to go. The ship turns and moves in that direction. Release the wheel to coast. On a keyboard, hold WASD or the arrow keys in the direction you want to travel.

Your chart only reveals what the crew can truly see. Visibility is limited by the height of the mast and the weather, while islands block the view beyond their shores.

Tap any charted town marker on the sea map or world chart to inspect its population, political factions, resources, trade routes, and market intelligence.

Your first merchant-prince milestone: complete three contracts, profit from a regional shortage, build influence with the Guild of Gilded Oars, and use that influence to charter a protected trade route.

Fair-market update: every port now posts separate buy and sell quotes with broker spreads and port duties, closing same-market round-trip exploits.

The world now sails without you: merchant vessels carry real cargo between ports, political crises alter production and prices, and paid informants can reveal opportunities before they become public news.

Encircling-world update: the chart now spans many seas and continents. Sailing beyond the eastern or western edge carries you around the globe to the opposite side; there is no artificial wall.

What can I do here?

Customs & jurisdiction

Honest declarations, permits, and good standing reduce risk. Forged papers and remote landings can evade restrictions, but repeat offenses bring confiscation, fines, political hostility, and port bans.

Market

Posted market: merchants charge a broker spread and port duties. The buy and sell quotes are different, so buying and immediately reselling in the same port always loses money.

Cargo plan

Assign individual lots to specialized compartments. Secured racks protect fragile goods, the dry locker slows spoilage, and smuggler’s lockers conceal contraband.

Port warehouse

Lease permanent storage at this port, then move individual cargo lots between the warehouse and your ship. Stored goods remain here between voyages and age at one quarter the rate of cargo at sea.

Local production chains

Workshops consume real market inputs each day. Supplying a missing input restores output and can relieve downstream shortages. Labor, fuel, infrastructure, resource depletion, and investment determine regional output and quality.

Contract board

Sealed contract cargo is loaded automatically and occupies hold space until delivery. Rival sponsors demand exclusive service, so accepting one commission can close another.

Whisper Network

Buy time-sensitive market, political, and shipping intelligence. Reports can reveal events before they reach the public gazette.

Shipyard & fleet

Purchase specialized vessels and fittings once, then change ships or refit freely while docked. Every choice carries a tradeoff.

Voyage readiness

Routes consume provisions, wear the ship, and test crew morale. Better quarters and storm fittings reduce those pressures.
