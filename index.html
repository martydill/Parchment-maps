The Gilded Archipelago

✦ ☙ ⚓ ❧ ✦

You inherit a narrow-hulled merchant vessel, a weathered chart, and a debt owed to the Crown of Avelorn.

Your voyage begins at Goldhaven, your home port on the coast of Avelorn.

To sail, drag the touch wheel toward the place you want the ship to go. The ship turns and moves in that direction. Release the wheel to coast. On a keyboard, hold WASD or the arrow keys in the direction you want to travel.

Your chart only reveals what the crew can truly see. Visibility is limited by the height of the mast and the weather, while islands block the view beyond their shores.

Tap any charted town marker on the sea map or world chart to inspect its population, political factions, resources, trade routes, and market intelligence.

Your first merchant-prince milestone: complete three contracts, profit from a regional shortage, build influence with the Guild of Gilded Oars, and use that influence to charter a protected trade route.

Fair-market update: every port now posts separate buy and sell quotes with broker spreads and port duties, closing same-market round-trip exploits.

The world now sails without you: merchant vessels carry real cargo between ports, political crises alter production and prices, and paid informants can reveal opportunities before they become public news.

Encircling-world update: the chart now spans many seas and continents. Sailing beyond the eastern or western edge carries you around the globe to the opposite side; there is no artificial wall.

A mobile-friendly prototype inspired by classic merchant-prince strategy games and hand-inked fantasy cartography.
