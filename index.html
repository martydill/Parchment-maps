Port warehouse

Lease permanent storage at this port, then move individual cargo lots between the warehouse and your ship. Stored goods remain here between voyages and age at one quarter the rate of cargo at sea.

Workshops consume real market inputs each day. Supplying a missing input restores output and can relieve downstream shortages. Labor, fuel, infrastructure, resource depletion, and investment determine regional output and quality.

Sealed contract cargo is loaded automatically and occupies hold space until delivery. Rival sponsors demand exclusive service, so accepting one commission can close another.
